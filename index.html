我们的故事 Our Story

🏮

Zhen Choo is more than a restaurant – it's our home. Locally owned and operated by a dedicated couple, we opened our doors in 2003 with a simple dream: to bring the true taste of authentic Chinese home cooking to the Pittsburgh community.

For over 20 years, we've had the privilege of serving generations of families, sharing meals, and creating lasting memories. Through the unprecedented challenges of COVID, we worked tirelessly to keep our doors open and our neighbors fed, never compromising on the quality or authenticity of our dishes.

Our commitment remains the same today – to provide a warm, inviting environment where every guest feels like family, and every bite tastes like tradition.
